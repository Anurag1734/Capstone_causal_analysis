# Phase 8 - Variable Roles

## Role policy

Roles below are provisional and treatment-specific. Availability in the master does not establish temporal eligibility or confounding status. `SubjectID`, `hadm_id`, and `study_id` are linkage/dependence fields, not ordinary adjustment variables.

| Variable | Source and actual field | Clinical meaning | Measurement time/window | Proposed role | Why plausible | Evidence | Remaining uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Age | Master: `anchor_age`; source concept: `patients.anchor_age` | Age at the MIMIC anchor year/admission representation | Patient/admission baseline, subject to cohort linkage | Candidate baseline confounder and effect modifier | Influences prescribing, cardiovascular risk, and death | Present in master; longitudinal audit describes source | Admission-specific linkage and age semantics in selected baseline not machine-validated here |
| Sex | Master: `sex_male`; source concept: `patients.gender` | Recorded sex/gender indicator | Fixed or baseline patient attribute | Candidate baseline confounder/effect modifier | May influence treatment decisions and MI risk | Present in master; source described in longitudinal audit | Coding and missing/unknown handling require validation |
| Diabetes | Master: `diabetes` | Prior-admission ICD-coded diabetes history | Intended pre-baseline history | Candidate baseline confounder | Affects statin indication and MI risk | Phase 6 and variable-role documentation | Exact lookback, completeness, and cutoff are not verified in executable local artifacts |
| Hypertension | Master: `hypertension` | Prior-admission hypertension history | Intended pre-baseline history | Candidate baseline confounder | Affects cardiovascular risk and treatment decisions | Phase 6 and variable-role documentation | Same history-window and completeness uncertainty |
| CKD | Master: `ckd` | Prior-admission chronic kidney disease history | Intended pre-baseline history | Candidate baseline confounder | Affects treatment selection, illness risk, and mortality | Phase 6 and variable-role documentation | Same history-window and completeness uncertainty |
| Prior MI | Master: `prior_mi` | Previous MI history | Must precede baseline admission | Candidate baseline confounder and eligibility factor | Strongly affects future MI risk and statin indication | Phase 6 and longitudinal design documents | Must confirm it excludes the baseline/future outcome admission |
| Presenting diagnosis/severity | No confirmed single master field; candidate source fields include diagnoses, vitals, labs, services | Acute clinical state and treatment indication | Baseline admission; exact pre-exposure timing required | Candidate confounder or selection variable | Acute context may influence both prescribing and future risk | Clinical rationale; source tables described in longitudinal audit | Not operationalized in the current Phase 8 design; many measures may be post-exposure |
| Cardiovascular burden/lipids | No confirmed field in current master; outpatient lipid data structurally limited | Atherosclerotic burden, dyslipidemia, CAD, heart failure | Should precede assignment | Important unmeasured confounder | Directly affects statin indication and MI risk | Clinical rationale; longitudinal audit notes missing outpatient pharmacy/lipid limitations | Likely insufficiently measured; residual confounding expected |
| ECG features | 95 engineered ECG fields; `ecg_time` | ECG-derived clinical representation | ECG time; relation to treatment assignment unresolved | Quarantined candidate covariate, not primary adjustment | Could measure baseline cardiovascular state if truly pre-treatment | Phase 1-5 audits | No defensible treatment-specific index ECG rule; could be post-treatment or event-proximal |
| ECG latent representation | `z_ecg_1` ... `z_ecg_64` | VAE representation of ECG | ECG time; provenance and treatment order unresolved | Quarantined; exclude from primary DAG/set | Predictiveness does not establish causal role | Phase 5 audit and variable-role documents | Training provenance, timing, and upstream clinical interpretation unresolved |
| Exposure | `statin_before` | Recent qualifying statin prescription/order indicator | Approx. 7-day pre-admission window bounded by prior discharge | Observed exposure proxy | It is the only available statin candidate with a defined historical window | `EXPOSURE_PROVENANCE.md`, `STATIN_DESIGN_DECISION.md` | Not administration, adherence, initiation, or exact assignment; prevalent-user mixture |
| Acute statin treatment | `statin_during` | In-hospital prescription/administration-derived indicator | During baseline admission; exact time unresolved | Post-origin/acute treatment or mediator candidate; not baseline exposure | May reflect acute care and severity | Phase 2/6 treatment-role audits | Could occur before or after an ECG/event; not valid for the primary baseline question |
| Post-discharge statin | `statin_after` | Post-discharge treatment indicator | After baseline discharge | Downstream variable; exclude from baseline adjustment | Cannot be a cause of the baseline admission state | Phase 2/6 treatment-role audits | Exact timing and relation to later outcome unresolved |
| Outcome | `is_MI` | Admission-level ICD-10 MI indicator | Outcome admission; must be a distinct later admission | Candidate observed outcome measurement | Available and admission-level | Master and cross-admission audit | Incident status, code validity, completeness outside ECG cohort, and horizon unresolved |
| MI timing proxy | `index_mi_time` | First qualifying troponin time proxy | Current admission/event-related | Outcome metadata only | Can support limited temporal checks | Phase 2 and temporal-gap audit | Not biological onset; null for 2,491 of 7,362 MI admissions in the cited master audit |
| Troponin label | `Label_Troponin` | Categorical cohort/temporal label | Admission/measurement-derived | Label metadata, not separate causal node | Useful for audit taxonomy | Phase 2/6 audits | Not a final incident outcome definition |
| Death | Source concept: `patients.dod`, `admissions.deathtime`, `hospital_expire_flag` | Death/competing event | During or after follow-up | Competing event and censoring determinant | Death prevents observing a later MI and may depend on exposure/risk | Medication report and longitudinal audit descriptions | Machine-readable local output absent; exact estimand handling not chosen |
| Observation end | Longitudinal audit: `observation_end` | End of observable follow-up | Post-baseline | Censoring/follow-up variable | Defines risk-set observation window | Described in longitudinal audit | Output absent in current workspace; completeness and administrative end need verification |
| IDs | `SubjectID`, `hadm_id`, `study_id` | Patient/admission/ECG identifiers | Administrative | Linkage, clustering, cohort construction | Needed for repeated-admission dependence and distinct-event logic | Master schema | Never treat as confounders or ordinary covariates |

## Missing or unresolved variables that matter most

The main omitted common causes are dyslipidemia/LDL, established coronary disease, heart failure, smoking, obesity/frailty, symptom trajectory, outpatient medication access, adherence, clinician treatment preference, and healthcare utilization. Some may be partially proxied, but the current evidence does not show that the candidate fields adequately capture them.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not adjust for `statin_during`, `statin_after`, vasopressor/ventilation indicators, future admissions, future ECGs, `index_mi_time`, `time_diff_hours`, or outcome labels. They are downstream, outcome-defined, time metadata, or temporally unresolved for the target total effect. Do not select ECG variables by predictive importance alone.
